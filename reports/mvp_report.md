## Notes and limitations

- The `mock-baseline` / `mock-large` registry entries are deterministic pipeline-testing tools, not scientific baselines.
- Cost estimates use a static price table (LLM backends) or 0.0 (mock / most non-LLM adapters); they are reporting aids, not billing.
- Failure taxonomy v2 is heuristic; `spatial_error` vs `symbol_error` is decided by a color-histogram comparison (see `src/failure_taxonomy.py`). `execution_error` covers API, subprocess, and HTTP failures alike.
- Heterogeneous solvers plug in via adapters — see `docs/SOLVER_ADAPTERS.md` and `configs/solvers.json`.
